What is tradie marketing
and do you need it?

Tradie marketing on the Sunshine Coast means getting your trade business visible in the places local customers are already looking - primarily Google Search and Google Maps. When a homeowner in Caloundra searches "plumber near me" or an owner in Buderim types "electrician Sunshine Coast", tradie marketing is what determines whether your name appears in the top three results or gets buried on page four.
