Common Issues We Find

What's usually
holding businesses back.

Wrong categories
Incorrect or missing primary and secondary categories are one of the most common ranking factors being missed.
Stale reviews
A burst of old reviews counts for much less than a steady stream of new ones. Recency matters more than total count.
Thin photos
Profiles with fewer than 10 photos signal low activity to Google and low professionalism to customers.
No Google Posts
Regular posts signal an active, engaged business. Most profiles we see have had no activity in months.
