Our Story

A Sunshine Coast business,
for Sunshine Coast businesses.

Haylo started with a simple belief: local service businesses do incredible work, and they deserve an online presence that reflects that. Most tradies and cleaners on the Sunshine Coast are brilliant at what they do - but getting found on Google feels like a different world entirely.

So we keep it focused. We do one thing well - helping local service businesses get into the top three on Google Maps and stay there. Clear reporting, no jargon, no lock-in contracts. Just a stronger local presence that brings calls in and keeps your calendar full.

We work with tradies, cleaners, and service businesses right across the Sunshine Coast - businesses built on hard work and word of mouth who are ready to let Google do some of the heavy lifting too.

Why It Matters

Your customers search Google
before they call anyone.

When a homeowner needs a plumber, a cleaner, or an electrician, they open Google Maps. They look at who comes up first, check the reviews, scan the photos, and call the one they feel good about. That decision happens quickly - and a strong, well-managed profile makes all the difference.

A Google Business Profile done properly is one of the most effective things a local service business can invest in. It's what we focus on every day, and we genuinely love helping businesses grow through it.

How We Work

Three things we never compromise on.

Results that show up.

Sunshine Coast only.
